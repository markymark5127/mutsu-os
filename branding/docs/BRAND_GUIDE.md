# Mutsu OS brand guide

This is the source of truth for new Mutsu artwork. Match these before adding icons, sounds, or copy.

## Voice

Calm, direct, and short. Mutsu is a desktop, not a product pitch. Use "Mutsu OS" in public names. Do not say Bazzite, Fedora, or "AI chatbot" on a user-facing surface.

## Colour

Japandi is the default. Cyberpunk is the night and high-contrast theme.

| Token | Japandi | Cyberpunk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#F3EFE7` | `#080C16` |
| Surface | `#FFFCF6` | `#0A1428` |
| Ink | `#232823` | `#E9F7FF` |
| Accent | `#788773` | `#00E5FF` |
| Deep | `#384B3C` | `#FF2D9B` |
| Line | `#D6DCCF` | `#34506B` |

## Type

The brand typeface is a humanist sans. The image prefers Inter, then Source Sans 3, then Cantarell, then Adwaita Sans. Do not ship a custom font file until it is licensed for redistribution.

## Icons

Rounded 46px corners on a 256px canvas. Sage fill, cream inset, one simple glyph. Folders stay sage. App icons use the same construction as `branding/icons/apps/`. Do not recolor third-party apps that we do not own.

## Sound

Short, quiet, single tones. No loops, no voice, no startup fanfare. The theme is `MutsuJapandi` and inherits `freedesktop`.

## Cursor

A sage pointer that inherits Adwaita for the cursors we have not drawn yet. Sources live in `branding/cursors/japandi/`.
